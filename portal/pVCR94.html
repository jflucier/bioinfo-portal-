If you are not redirected automatically, follow this procedure to view data:
aligned reads for ChIP-exo and RNA-seq can be visualized using the UCSC genome browser at http://genome.ucsc.edu/cgi-bin/hgHubConnect before selecting the "My Hubs" tab, pasting the following URL http://ucscbrowser.genap.ca/hub/pskjnlwkefwserfvwwecwf/Rodrigue_lab/pVCR94deltaX_hub.txt and pressing the "Add Hub" button. The data will be displayed upon pressing the "Genomes" tab at the top left corner of the UCSC genome browser, selecting "pVCR94deltaX_v1" in the "group" drop-down menu, and clicking on "submit".

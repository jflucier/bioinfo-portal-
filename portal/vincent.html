Investigation of whole-genome cloning and transplantation using the quasi-minimal bacterium Mesoplasma florum

Sequence of the Mesoplasma florum L1 cloned in Saccharomyces cerevisiae in fasta format as well as in partially annotated GenBank. This sequence was confirmed by sequencing the S. cerevisiae M-his-florum strain described in: V. Baby, F. Labroussaa, J. Brodeur, D. Matteau, G. Gourgues, C. Lartigue and S. Rodrigue, Investigation of whole-genome cloning and transplantation using the quasi-minimal bacterium Mesoplasma florum, [under review]

If you are not redirected automatically, follow this procedure to view data:
aligned reads for SXT ChIP-exo, RNA-seq and 5'-RACE, as well as ChIP-exo from R391, ICEVflInd1, and MGIVflInd1, can be visualized using the UCSC genome browser at http://genome.ucsc.edu/cgi-bin/hgHubConnect before selecting the "My Hubs" tab, pasting the following URL https://datahub-101-cw2.p.genap.ca/Laprade_2015/SetCD_hub.txt and pressing the "Add Hub" button. The data will be displayed upon pressing the "Genomes" tab at the top left corner of the UCSC genome browser, selecting "SXT", "R391", "ICEVflInd1" or "MGIVflInd1" in the "group" drop-down menu, and clicking on "submit".

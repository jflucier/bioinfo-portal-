SetCD activator

This link will automatically redirect to a SetCD activator data pre-loaded UCSC genome browser hosted on the Genap.ca server at the Université de Sherbrooke in January 2015. In the meantime, aligned reads for SXT ChIP-exo, RNA-seq and 5'-RACE, as well as ChIP-exo from R391, ICEVflInd1, and MGIVflInd1, can be visualized using the UCSC genome browser at http://genome.ucsc.edu/cgi-bin/hgHubConnect before selecting the "My Hubs" tab, pasting the following URL http://ucscbrowser.genap.ca/hub/pskjnlwkefwserfvwwecwf/Rodrigue_lab/SetCD_hub.txt and pressing the "Add Hub" button. The data will be displayed upon pressing the "Genomes" tab at the top left corner of the UCSC genome browser, selecting "SXT", "R391", "ICEVflInd1" or "MGIVflInd1" in the "group" drop-down menu, and clicking on "submit".

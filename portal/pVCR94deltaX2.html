If you are not redirected automatically, follow this procedure to view data:
Genome-wide visualization of transposon insertion profiles following conjugative transfer of the pVCR94Sp mutant library into Vibrio cholerae O395 or its restriction-deficient ΔhsdR derivative can be accessed at: http://genome.ucsc.edu/cgi-bin/hgHubConnect before selecting the "Connected Hubs" tab, pasting the following URL https://g-f1a0a.ad336.08cc.data.globus.org/Tn-Seq_pVCR94deltaX2/Tn-Seq_pVCR94deltaX2.hub.txt and pressing the "Add Hub" button. The data will be displayed upon pressing the "Genomes" tab at the top left corner of the UCSC genome browser, selecting "pVCR94deltaX2" in the "Find Position" drop-down menu, and clicking on "GO".
